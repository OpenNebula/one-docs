## Headings and Titles

For document titles, headings, sub-headings, tables and figures, use Title case.

For headings, use Title case. Capitalize:

* The first letter in the title  
* The first letter of any major word  
* The first letter of a minor word that’s four or more letters long  
* The first letter after an em dash ( — ), colon ( : ) or period

Don’t capitalize the second word in a hyphenated compound word, such as Front-end.

Major words are: Nouns, pronouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, and all words more than four letters long.

Minor words are:

* Articles (“the,” “a,” “an,”)  
* Short prepositions (such as “as,” “at,” “in,” “on,” “to”)  
* Short conjunctions (such as “as,” “and,” “for,” “if”)

There are several capitalization styles in the English language, even within “US English.” If in doubt, please refer to capitalization as used in the [APA Guide](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/capitalization/title-case).

## Terminology

OpenNebula docs including technical and promotional material usually capitalize certain concepts, especially if they are mentioned repeatedly in a document. Examples include:

* Front-end  
* Virtual Machines  
* Clusters  
* Hosts  
* Service Network  
* Storage Network  
* Virtual Network 
* Provider
* Provision 
* Concepts or sets of principles that are referenced several times throughout the documentation, such as the OpenNebula Reference Architecture

## Branding

Capitalization should be also be respected for products and brands within the OpenNebula ecosystem, including internal components, such as OneForm or OneDeploy, as well as external tools used with OpenNebula, such as NetApp or AlmaLinux. Please refer to the [Branding Guide](https://github.com/OpenNebula/website/wiki/Branding) for more details.
