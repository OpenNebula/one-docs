OpenNebula can be integrated with storage solutions provided by technology partners. The integrations in this section are developed, maintained, and supported by their respective providers; they are not covered by the OpenNebula Subscription. OpenNebula Support continues to cover OpenNebula components according to the customer's subscription. Refer to each provider's documentation for installation, compatibility, lifecycle, and support information.
