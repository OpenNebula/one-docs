This AI Factory Proof of Concept guides take the user on a tour of OpenNebula's AI-ready capabilities. As part of the OpenNebula AI Factory PoC process, you will be provided with access to a hosted infrastructure that allows you to explore OpenNebula's AI capabilities in a sandbox environment. 

You will be provided with details and credentials for connecting to the VPN to connect to the hosted infrastructure by your OpenNebula PoC contact. Please follow the [Connecting via VPN Guide]({{% relref "solutions/engineering_blueprints/ai_poc/connecting/" %}}) with these credentials in hand to start the PoC. 

Once you have connected to the infrastructure, start following the guides in this section step by step. The guides in this section take you through the following capabilities (follow the guides in order):

* [Managing permissions through users and groups with OpenNebula's multi-tenancy model]({{% relref "solutions/engineering_blueprints/ai_poc/access_permissions/" %}})
* [Deploying a LLM with a GPU-enabled Virtual Machine through the vLLM framework](({{% relref "solutions/engineering_blueprints/ai_poc/gpu_vm/" %}}))
* [Deploying a managed, GPU-enabled Kubernetes Cluster with OneKS](({{% relref "solutions/engineering_blueprints/ai_poc/gpu_vm/" %}}))
