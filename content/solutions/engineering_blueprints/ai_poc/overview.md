The AI Factory Guided Evaluation allows potential customers to test the AI-ready capabilities of the OpenNebula platform on hosted infrastructure provided by OpenNebula as part of a Proof of Concept (PoC) process. The PoC process provides a low-risk opportunity to assess OpenNebula's functionality, performance, and compatibility, ensuring it meets specific needs and requirements before committing to a full deployment.

The guides in this section take the user on a structured tour through OpenNebula's advanced AI capabilities. 

## How it Works

If you are interested in evaluating OpenNebula's AI capabilities through the PoC service, [contact the OpenNebula sales team](https://opennebula.io/contact/) to start the process. Once you have started the PoC process, the OpenNebula team will set up the hosted infrastructure with OpenNebula pre-installed and pre-configured. Your PoC contact will send you instructions and credentials to connect to the hosted infrastructure and access the hosted OpenNebula cloud.

After receiving instructions and credentials from your OpenNebula PoC contact, you should follow the guides in this section in the order given below:

* [Explore OpenNebula's User Access Management Funcionality]({{% relref "solutions/engineering_blueprints/ai_poc/access_permissions/" %}})
* [Deploying an LLM on a GPU-enabled Virtual Machine]({{% relref "solutions/engineering_blueprints/ai_poc/gpu_vm/" %}})
