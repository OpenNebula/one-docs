---
title: "Integration Blueprints"
description: "Integration blueprints for using OpenNebula with external technologies."
weight: 4
---

These blueprints describe the use of OpenNebula with an external technology that is not part of the OpenNebula product support and continuous validation lifecycle. The OpenNebula Subscription covers the OpenNebula components and associated OpenNebula integration points. Support, maintenance, compatibility, and lifecycle of the external technology and any externally maintained integration components remain the responsibility of their respective provider or project, unless otherwise stated.
