Organizations today are looking for flexible, open solutions to simplify their IT infrastructure, consolidate workloads, and build scalable private, hybrid, and edge clouds. By adopting a unified cloud management and virtualization platform, they can reduce operational complexity, lower costs (TCO), and gain complete control over their digital infrastructure.

OpenNebula is a mature, open platform that provides cloud and virtualization management, combining hypervisor management, cloud orchestration, and hybrid and edge cloud provisioning in a single lightweight solution.

OpenNebula offers:

* A predictable subscription model for enterprise maintenance and support of an open source platform.
* Unified virtualization and cloud management.
* Integrated edge and hybrid cloud provisioning through OneForm, including supported public cloud and bare-metal providers such as AWS.
* Integrated elastic Kubernetes-as-a-Service with OneKS and its application catalog.
* Enterprise-grade scalability and automation, with high availability, multi-tenancy, and federation across sites.

The following guide summarizes OpenNebula {{< version >}} capabilities to support product comparisons and the preparation of Requests for Proposal (RFPs). It covers three areas:

These organizations benefit from an agile, secure, and future-ready cloud infrastructure that gives them complete control over their technology stack. Choose OpenNebula as your cloud solution.

| **Bare Metal as a Service** | <p style="margin-bottom: 0px">NVIDIA NICo integration provisions, monitors, and deletes bare-metal instances through OpenNebula templates, users, groups, and quotas. NICo supplies the underlying hardware capacity and instance lifecycle operations.</p> |
