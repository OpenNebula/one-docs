[Open OnDemand](https://openondemand.org/) gives HPC users a browser interface to a cluster: a file browser, a shell, job submission and interactive applications such as JupyterLab, RStudio, Octave and VS Code. The Open OnDemand appliance runs it on OpenNebula as a OneFlow service, with a portal, a shared home and a pool of compute VMs that grows and shrinks with the number of open sessions.
